## Equipment text

`content/equipment_catalogs/srd_equipment.json` reproduces the equipment
entries of SRD 5.2.1 (name, category, cost, weight, description). The text was
transcribed through the open5e API (https://api.open5e.com, document
`srd-2024`) because dnd5eapi's 2024 equipment records carry no description at
all, and is redistributed under the same CC-BY-4.0 terms as the SRD.
Regenerate with `scripts/rulesets/import_srd_equipment_text.py`.
